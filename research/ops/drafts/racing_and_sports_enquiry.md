What I'm after (Australia-wide, all states and territories, thoroughbreds):

5. Fields and acceptances: final fields, scratchings with times, emergencies.
6. Coverage and depth: all Australian meetings (incl. NT, TAS and country), historical back to October 2022, then daily updates.

- Pricing for historical (Oct 2022 onward) plus ongoing daily access, and any trial or sample.
- Rate limits or technical requirements.

We don't need betting odds or market data. Our assessments are kept fully independent of the market.
