# Pre-registered tests (written 25 Nov 2026, before any race of 26 Nov)

Purpose: the Batch 8-9 candidates were chosen after looking at 2025-26, so 2025-26 cannot confirm them. These tests use
ONLY races run from 26 Nov 2026 onward, scored with the information available before each race. Nothing below is in
production; the official engine of each day is the baseline. Definitions are fixed here and will not be changed.

Baseline: the official engine's saved pre-race prediction for each race (v2.1 Qld / v2.0 NSW-Vic-ACT as at 25 Nov).
Measure: log-likelihood of the winner per race, candidate minus baseline; 95% interval by meeting bootstrap (2,000 draws).
Candidate coefficient: fitted ONCE on races before 2025-01-01 (as in research/batch9/scripts), then frozen.
Decision: a candidate passes if the interval excludes zero on at least 1,000 races (NSW/Vic) or 400 races (Qld);
it is rejected if the upper end is below +0.001; otherwise the test continues. Negative results are kept.

| ID | Region | Candidate term (on top of the baseline) | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P9-B1 | NSW/Vic/ACT | Proven ceiling after a long slide | (best pre-race rating so far - today's rating) x [6+ measured runs since that best] |
| P9-B2 | NSW/Vic/ACT | Recent form counts less for established horses | average finishing percentile of the last 3 x [15+ runs in our history] |
| P9-F1 | NSW/Vic/ACT and Qld separately | Class-of-track step | track level today minus last start (country 0, provincial 1, metro 2) |
| P9-C1 | Qld | Real late acceleration last start | own speed rose over the last 200m (> 0.1 m/s) and by 0.3 m/s more than the race median |

Data cut-off for definitions: history to 23 Nov 2026 (master-2026-11-23), later weekly versions as published.
