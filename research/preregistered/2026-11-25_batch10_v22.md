# Pre-registered prospective check: Engine v2.2 (written 25 Nov 2026, before any 26 Nov race)
- Races: every NSW, VIC, ACT, SA, WA and TAS race rated by the daily run from 26 Nov 2026, field >= 6, one winner.
- Compare: v2.2 chance (v2 prediction files, engine_release 'Engine v2.2') vs the v2.0 score for the same runners (recomputed with ENGINE_V22=0 from the same saved inputs).
- Primary: mean log-likelihood of the winner per race, v2.2 minus v2.0, meeting-bootstrap 95% CI. Expectation from research: about +0.01 (2025-26: +0.0125).
- Secondary: top-pick win rate difference (expected about 0); SA/WA/TAS separately (expected larger gain); calibration of the v2.2 display chance.
- First look after 300 races; decision review after 4 weeks. No other outcome will be reported as "the" result.
