## Variants (national rating and national ability figures rebuilt from the same master database, master-2026-11-23-sa-wa-tas-qld)
- S0 baseline: figures convert beaten lengths with 2.4 m / race average speed (about 0.143 s per length); weight credit 1.0 kg per kg.
- S1: figures convert at a fixed 0.170 s per length (Queensland tracking value); weight credit 1.0. The margin-based rating is unchanged
  (it never converts lengths to seconds), so S1 can only act through the ability figures (a_rec, a_best3, opposition faced).
- W050 / W075: weight credit 0.5 / 0.75 kg per kg above or below 57 kg, in BOTH the margin-based rating and the figures; conversion as S0.
  Today's weight effect is re-estimated in the refit (weight vs field), so history and today stay consistent.

## Test
Whole national model refitted per variant (v1 inputs + Engine v2 terms; v2.2 terms left out because they were built on the baseline rating),
same code as batch11/s11_simplify.py: (A) fit before 2024, judge 2024; (B) fit before 2025, judge 2025-26. Paired per-race log-likelihood
vs S0 with a meeting bootstrap; top-pick rate reported alongside.

## Decision rule (fixed now)
A variant is "supported" only if: window B gain > 0 with the 95% interval above 0, AND window A gain > 0, AND top-pick change not below
-0.5 points in either window. Among the weight credits, 0.5 or 0.75 replaces 1.0 only if it is supported (two comparisons: noted, no
correction claimed). A supported variant becomes a release candidate, shadow-scored before any release. Anything else: recorded as
rejected or inconclusive in research/LIBRARY.md.

## Not tested here
