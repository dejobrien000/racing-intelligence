Experience a Day at the Races at Gawler & Barossa Jockey Club

Enjoy the perfect blend of country charm and racing excitement at the Gawler & Barossa Jockey Club – one of South Australia’s premier racing destinations.

Located just 42 km north of Adelaide in the heart of wine country, the Club offers a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere, with the added convenience of its own dedicated railway station. If you’re planning to travel by train, catch the Gawler Central line (non-express) for direct access to the racecourse.

Race day at Gawler is more than just thrilling thoroughbred action. From the moment you arrive, you’ll be greeted by lush lawns, blooming rose gardens and the striking Terrace Function Centre – the ideal spot to enjoy a delicious lunch while overlooking the track.

With a range of food and beverage options, a full bar and TAB facilities, and spacious indoor and outdoor areas, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

Whether you’re a racing enthusiast, looking for a fun day out, or simply wanting to soak up the local hospitality, a day at Gawler & Barossa Jockey Club is always a memorable experience.

 

Ticketing & Hospitality Options

Enjoy the freedom to explore the racecourse, relax trackside on the lawns, soak up the atmosphere, place a bet and get close to the racing action.

Bring your picnic rug and settle in trackside, or enjoy the casual seating and easy access to our budget-friendly food options from the canteen and BBQ.

General Admission is the perfect choice for a relaxed and affordable day out with friends or family.

Terrace Function Centre

Upgrade your race day experience in the Club’s signature indoor venue, the Terrace Function Centre.

Featuring floor-to-ceiling windows and a raised viewing area, the Terrace offers uninterrupted views of the track. Enjoy reserved table seating and a delicious, ever-changing two-course bistro menu, along with full bar and TAB facilities, all while being protected from the weather.

This option is perfect for those wanting to enjoy the races in style and comfort. To guarantee your spot, tickets must be purchased in advance, particularly for feature race days.

Wolf Blass Pavilion

Positioned perfectly between the mounting yard and home straight, the Wolf Blass Pavilion offers a relaxed yet elevated race day experience.

With both undercover and open-air spaces, guests can enjoy exclusive bar access, casual seating and prime views of the horses in action.

Ideal for group bookings, celebrations or corporate hospitality, the Pavilion provides a fantastic social setting just steps from the track.

Whether you're a seasoned racegoer or a first-time visitor, there's a race day experience to suit every style and budget.

Come and enjoy the excitement of live racing, the warmth of country hospitality and the beautiful surrounds of Gawler – where great days are made.

Tickets & Information

All hospitality and reserved race day packages must be purchased online through Moshtix. Pricing and availability for hospitality options can be viewed via the Buy Tickets button.

For any questions, please email events@gawlerjockeyclub.com.au or call (08) 8522 1801.

Please note that BYO alcohol is not permitted anywhere on the premises. However, BYO food is welcome, except inside the Terrace Function Centre dining area, where outside food is not permitted.
